Take your practice with you

Study anywhere.

Save Python and your lessons on this iPad. Once the download is ready, you can practise without Wi-Fi or a hotspot.

First, set up your iPad

  1. In Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Keep “Open as Web App” on if shown.
  2. Open the new Python icon and sign in while connected. Open Study offline here and download inside that app.
  3. Keep this screen open until it says Ready offline. Then turn Wi-Fi off and try a lesson and a code cell.
Downloads belong to this browser or installed app on this device. Downloading on your phone does not prepare your iPad.
