B Study the concepts

Open each card to read it and update your progress.

A cell is identified by its column letter and row number. B2 means column B, row 2. A colon joins the ends of a continuous range: B2:B4 includes B2, B3 and B4.

Find cell B3 in the practice worksheet. Its starting value is 8.

Excel formulas begin with =. Enter =SUM(B2:B4) in B5 to add the numeric values from the three cells.

Change B3 from 8 to 10 and apply the formula. The total becomes 27. Real Excel normally recalculates automatically; this preview uses an Apply button.

C Practice checklist

D Test your knowledge

Q1. Which range includes B2, B3 and B4?
B2:B4 stays in column B and spans rows 2 to 4.
Q2. What is the sum of 12, 8 and 5?
12 + 8 + 5 = 25.
