Turn your business summary into a chart

Compare amounts, count records by status and build a Ready-only report. Learn to communicate the measure, unit and scope, not just the picture.

From source records to a business report

In Excel, first summarize the data using a PivotTable or formulas. Select the summary categories and values, excluding the grand total, and choose Insert → Column or Bar Chart. Give the chart a meaningful title and label its units.

A column chart uses vertical bars; a bar chart uses horizontal bars. A zero baseline keeps length comparisons honest, including for negative amounts. Count records rather than summing their amounts when your question is about how many records there are.

A filtered report must say which records were included. Read the largest category from the chart or its data table; if the values tie, do not claim a single winner. The report builder below is a browser simulation, not the native Excel chart editor.
