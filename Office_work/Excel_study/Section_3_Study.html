A $ locks the part immediately after it. It does not format a number as money. You can type the dollar signs directly, including on an iPad keyboard.

In Microsoft Excel, enter the first formula and press Enter. Copy that cell and paste into the destination cells, or drag its fill handle. Check the resulting references before using the totals.

Formulas

This practice supports two cell references joined by * or / within A1:F12, with numeric inputs up to ±1 trillion. Blank cells calculate as zero; division by zero needs correction.
