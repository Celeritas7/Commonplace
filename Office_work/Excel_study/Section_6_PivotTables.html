Group your business records with PivotTables

Summarize a register by destination or status, arrange a cross-tab, and filter a report without deleting the original records.

Source data → field layout → summary

In Microsoft Excel, select your source range (including headers), then choose Insert → PivotTable. In the field list, place categories in Rows and Columns, amounts or record labels in Values, and a report filter in Filters.

Choose Sum of Amount to add numeric values. Choose Count of Record to count nonblank record labels, including a record whose amount is zero. A blank intersection means no record matched that combination; zero is a valid amount for a matching record.

A PivotTable summarizes a source snapshot. After editing source values, use Refresh to read them again. Changing a field layout or report filter does not delete rows from the source.
