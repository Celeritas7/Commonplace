Tapers p.137–138

Standard tapers for general engineering (IS 3458-1966). Taper is expressed as 1:x; the half-angle α/2 sets the sine-bar value per 100 mm. Steeper tapers (1:0.289 … 1:5) for centres, seals and self-release tool fittings; shallow self-holding tapers (1:10 … 1:50, Morse) for tool shanks and spindle noses.

Minimum bending radii — sheets & tubes p.158–159

Cold forming/bending of flat rolled sheet & strip (DIN 6935-1969). The minimum inside bend radius R depends on material, tensile strength and thickness s; in the bend the thickness reduces by ≈20%.

Rules
  • Values apply for bending angle α ≤ 120°; for α > 120° use the next-higher thickness range.
  • Radii are for bending at right angles to the rolling direction (preferred). For steel ≤65 kgf/mm² with s ≥ 3 and bending parallel to rolling, use higher values.
  • Round the chosen R to a preferred radius (see Radii & chamfers above).
Length before bending  L = a + b + V V is a compensating value from the bend geometry; for β ≤ 90°, V = π·[(180−β)/180]·(R + s·k/2) − 2(R + s); correction factor k from R/s (0.65 at R/s≈1 up to 1 at R/s≈4).
