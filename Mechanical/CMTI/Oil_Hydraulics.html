Working relations p.787–800

F = p·A    Q = A·v    P (kW) = p(bar)·Q(l/min) / 600·η Cylinder force from pressure × piston area; speed from flow ÷ area. η ≈ 0.80–0.90 overall for a pump-valve-cylinder chain.

Valves p.831–880

  • Pressure control: relief (system safety), reducing (lower branch pressure), sequence (order of operations), counterbalance (hold vertical loads).
  • Flow control: throttle (load-dependent) and pressure-compensated (constant speed); meter-out preferred for machine-tool feeds — holds against running-away loads.
  • Direction control: spool valves named ways/positions (4/2, 4/3…); centre conditions — closed (holds), tandem (unloads pump), float. Check valves incl. pilot-operated for load holding.

Circuit building blocks p.840–879

  • Reciprocating table drive (grinders): 4/3 valve + pilot reversing dogs, meter-out flow control, cushioned cylinder ends.
  • Rapid-approach / slow-feed: hi-lo twin pump, or bypass throttle cut in by a cam-operated valve.
  • Clamping: reducing valve + pilot-operated check holds the part with the pump unloaded; pressure switch interlocks the cycle start.
  • Counterbalance under vertical rams; accumulator for leakage make-up and shock damping.
