Ra, Rz & sampling length p.513–515

Ra (centre-line average) is the mean deviation of the profile from its mean line over the sampling length; Rz is the ten-point height — mean distance between the five highest peaks and five deepest valleys.

Ra = (1/l) · ∫0l |y(x)| dx l = sampling (cut-off) length. As a first guide Rz ≈ 4·Ra for machined surfaces.
