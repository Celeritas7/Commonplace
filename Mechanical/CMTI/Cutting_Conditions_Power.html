Cutting force & power p.637–653

Fc = ks·a·f    Pcut (kW) = Fc(kgf)·v(m/min) / 6120    Pmotor = Pcut / η a = depth of cut (mm), f = feed (mm/rev), ks = specific cutting pressure, η ≈ 0.7–0.85 for the drive train. Feed force ≈ 0.3–0.4 Fc, radial ≈ 0.2–0.4 Fc.

Tool life — Taylor p.659–661

v · Tn = C T = tool life (min). Exponent n: HSS 0.10–0.15 · brazed carbide 0.20–0.30 · ceramic 0.4. Doubling speed cuts HSS life ≈ 100-fold but carbide only ≈ 10-fold — carbide tolerates speed, not interruption.
Economic life

Typical target: T ≈ 30–60 min for manual machines, shorter (10–15 min) where tool change is quick and machine time expensive.
