§01 Installation Practice · Sheet WF-04

Wire Replacement Criteria

SOURCE — WIRING FUNDAMENTALS COURSE (AC 43.13-1B PRACTICE)

Wire is not repaired by hope. When inspection finds any of these conditions, the wire comes out — a checklist of nine replace-when conditions, plus two hard rules on shielding for the replacement.

REPLACE THE WIRE WHEN —
01Chafed or frayed
02Insulation suspected of being penetrated
03Outer insulation is cracking
04Damaged by — or known exposure to — electrolyte, oil, hydraulic fluid, etc.
05Evidence of overheating can be seen
06Evidence of being crushed or kinked
07Shield on shielded wire frayed and/or corroded
08Breaks, cracks, dirt or moisture in plastic sleeving
09Splices occurring at less than 10-ft intervals along a section
SHIELDING REQUIREMENTS
Match the shield

Replacement wires must have the same shielding characteristics as the original — shield optical coverage and resistance per unit length.

Stay inside the shield

Replacement wires should not be installed outside the bundle shield — a wire riding on the outside defeats the shield and chafes against it.

✗ ADDING WIRES TO A BUNDLE — WATCH FOR
Chafing from the incorrect procedure: a new wire laid loosely over the bundle instead of being routed with it rubs at every clamp and crossover point.
