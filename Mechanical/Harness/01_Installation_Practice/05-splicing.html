§01 Installation Practice · Sheet WF-05

Splicing Practice

SOURCE — WIRING FUNDAMENTALS COURSE (AC 43.13-1B PRACTICE)

Every splice is a resistance point, a stiffness discontinuity, and a bulge in the bundle. The discipline is simple: splice rarely, splice where you can see it, and never let two splices sit side by side.

01
Keep to a minimum — a splice is a defect you chose to install.
02
Avoid high-vibration areas — vibration works the joint and fatigues strands at the barrel mouth.
03
Locate to permit inspection — a splice buried in lacing can't be checked for overheating.
04
Stagger in bundles — offset splice positions along the run to minimise the increase in bundle diameter.
05
Use self-insulated splice connectors where possible — no separate sleeving step to get wrong.
FIG. WF-05 — STAGGERED SPLICES
Splices offset along the run — bundle diameter grows by one barrel, not three
✓ GANGED SPLICES — PROPER STRAIN RELIEF
Ganged wire splices (e.g. diode packs) supported so the wires enter without tension — the joint carries current, the support carries load.
✗ PRE-LOAD CONDITION
Ganged splices installed with the wires already under tension — every vibration cycle adds to a joint that starts life loaded. Watch also for overheating at the splice: discoloured insulation is a failed crimp telling on itself.
