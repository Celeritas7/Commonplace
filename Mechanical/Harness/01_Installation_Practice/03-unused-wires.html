§01 Installation Practice · Sheet WF-03

Unused Wires · Coil & Stow

SOURCE — WIRING FUNDAMENTALS COURSE (AC 43.13-1B PRACTICE)

A dead wire is never just left hanging. An exposed conductor on a "spare" is a live fault waiting for a ground path. Three requirements, then a stowing method matched to the vibration environment.

RULES — SECURING UNUSED WIRES
01
Secured — tied into the bundle or secured to a permanent structure. Never free to whip against adjacent runs.
02
Individually cut — strands trimmed even with the insulation. No copper whiskers past the jacket.
03
Insulated end — a pre-insulated closed-end connector, or a 1-inch piece of insulating tubing folded back over the end and tied.
✗ TYPICAL DEFECT
Improper termination with exposed conductor — should be properly capped (insulated) and secured to the bundle. A spare connector contact gets a single-contact attachment tied to the wire bundle with tying tape.
FIG. WF-03 — COIL & STOW METHODS
Short bundle · low vibration
Coil, tie the coil with bundle ties, clamp to structure.
Long bundle · low vibration
Excess wire folded into a flat coil, tied at multiple points, clamped.
Medium / high vibration
Wrap the coil in Teflon tape and tie it to an adjacent wire bundle.
