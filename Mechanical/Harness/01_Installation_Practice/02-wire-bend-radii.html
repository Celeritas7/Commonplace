§01 Installation Practice · Sheet WF-02

Wire Bend Radii

SOURCE — WIRING FUNDAMENTALS COURSE (AC 43.13-1B PRACTICE)

Bend a wire too tight and the conductor work-hardens, insulation stretches thin on the outside of the bend, and shields open up. The rules are simple multiples of the outside diameter of the largest wire or cable in the group — one base rule, three exceptions.

10×
Base rule
Minimum bend radius for an unsupported wire or bundle — 10× the OD of the largest wire or cable in the group.
3×
Supported loop
At terminations or where the bundle reverses direction, supported at both ends of the loop.
6×
RF cables
Coax geometry is the signal path — a tight bend changes impedance permanently.
20×
Thermocouple
Cold-working thermocouple wire shifts its calibration — keep it nearly straight.
FIG. WF-02 — MINIMUM BEND RADII
r ≥ 10×D
No support at end of bend — 10× OD of largest wire
r ≥ 3×D
Loop supported at both ends — 3× diameter allowed
✓ BEND RADII OKAY
Loop greater than 3× diameter, secured at both ends of the loop — the clamps carry the load, not the copper.
✗ BEND RADII PROBLEM
Loop tighter than 3× the diameter — insulation stressed on the outer face, conductor strands fatiguing at the bend root.
