§05 Circuits & Protection · Sheet CP-02

Overload Protection

SOURCE — CALTRANS ELECTRIC STANDARD §3 · FMCSR 49 §393.31

The protective device guards the wire, not the load — it must open before the conductor’s insulation cooks. That single idea generates every placement and sizing rule on this sheet.

125%
Sizing ceiling
If capacity falls between standard ratings, the next larger device is allowed — up to 125% of the conductor’s ampacity, never beyond.
72”
Battery main fuse
Every positive battery output gets a trip-free breaker or fuse, as close to the battery as practical — at most 72” away, and then only in loom inside an enclosure.
7”/40”
Branch fuses
Each branch fuses at its power source — within 7” along the conductor, or 40” if the run stays in loom inside an enclosure. Branch fuses open before main fuses.

✓ PLACEMENT CHECKLIST
Readily accessible · labeled with circuit + rating · clear of mechanical damage, ignitable material and explosive vapour · voltage rating ≥ circuit voltage · protective device on the load side of the main power switch.
✗ THE GROUNDED-CONDUCTOR TRAP
Never fuse only the grounded (return) conductor — a device in series with a grounded conductor must open all load conductors simultaneously, or the circuit stays live through the fault.
