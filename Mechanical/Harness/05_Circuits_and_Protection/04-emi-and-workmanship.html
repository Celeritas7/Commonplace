§05 Circuits & Protection · Sheet CP-04

EMI & Workmanship

SOURCE — CALTRANS ELECTRIC STANDARD §5, §7

Noise reaches a victim circuit by exactly three roads — radiation, conduction, induction — and every fix on the menu blocks one of them. The rest of this sheet is the quality floor: marking and workmanship rules that make a harness inspectable years later.

Radiated
Through the air
Radio transmitters, ignition. Counter with shielding, ferrite cores, grounding.
Conducted
Down the wire
Shared supply and ground paths. Counter with filters, suppressors at the source, isolated circuits.
Induced
Wire to wire
Magnetic or capacitive coupling in a shared bundle. Counter with separation, rerouting, twisted pairs, shields.
⚠ THE ONE ROUTING RULE
Signal, data and control conductors never run adjacent to DC power conductors. Known noise-makers — alternator output, ignition, solenoids, electric fuel pumps, inverters, fluorescent lighting — get their own routing; if separation is impossible, shield or suppress. Nothing electronic on the circuit while cranking, unless isolated.
TABLE CP-04 — MARKING & WORKMANSHIP FLOOR (§5)
AreaRequirement
Wire markingOne colour per circuit, source to load; connectors marked so cross-connection is impossible; switches and controls labeled by function.
CleanlinessNo smudges, spattered solder, metal chips or mold release left after fabrication.
FastenersNo cross-threading; 1.5 thread pitches proud minimum; single-fastener failure must not free a part; friction alone never retains anything.
ServiceabilityNo unsoldering to reach terminals or mounting screws; replaceable parts never riveted or potted in; spare fuses and plug-in relays supplied installed.
AcceptanceIPC-A-620 Class 2 is the inspection yardstick for every cable and harness assembly.
✓ SUPPRESSION MENU
Resistors · capacitors & inductors · interference filters · ferrite cores · diodes · shielding · grounding · separation & rerouting — matched to the vehicle and the noise source.
✗ ADD-ON AMNESIA
Auxiliary systems must add zero EMF effect to the vehicle. The radio that buzzes only with the new beacon on? That’s the retrofit failing this clause — fix it at the source, not the radio.
