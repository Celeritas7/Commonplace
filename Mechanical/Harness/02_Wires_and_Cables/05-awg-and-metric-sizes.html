SOURCE — JUDD WIRE AUTOMOTIVE CATALOG · CONDUCTOR & CONVERSION REFERENCE PP. 2–3, 10

⚠ EQUIVALENT ≠ IDENTICAL
20 AWG (1072 cmil min) and 0.5 mm² (0.508 mm² min) pass different minimum-area rules — a part qualified to one spec doesn’t automatically meet the other. Check the governing spec column on the drawing before substituting. Stranding also shifts within a size: the same 0.5 mm² comes as 7, 19, 20, 41 or 266 strands depending on flex duty.
