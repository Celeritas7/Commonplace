PIDG & PIDG FASTON

SOURCE — AMP CATALOG 82042 · PIDG PP. 7–32 · PIDG FASTON PP. 33–36

PIDG — Pre-Insulated Diamond Grip — is the premium family: the one qualified to MIL-T-7928 and the one you reach for in vibration, shock and critical circuits. PIDG FASTON is the same construction ending in a push-on tab receptacle instead of a stud tongue.

PIDG
Crimped stud terminals & splices
Tin-plated copper body (phosphor bronze for spring spades) · copper support sleeve · nylon insulation.
300 V · 105 °C · MIL-T-7928 Type II, Class 1 & 2
Ranges 22-16 · 16-14 · 12-10 AWG, solid or stranded. Butt “window” splices let you inspect wire insertion.
PIDG FASTON
Push-on “250” series · TETRA-CRIMP tooling
Brass or phosphor bronze body, unplated or tin-plated · same copper sleeve + nylon.
300 V · 105 °C · UL310
Ranges 22-18 · 16-14 · 12-10 AWG, stranded. Disconnectable — mates a .250” tab; line-splice connectors available.

TABLE TS-02 — WHEN TO USE WHICH
SituationPick

Component swaps expectedPIDG FASTON — disconnect without touching a fastener
Permanent wire-to-wire jointPIDG butt window splice — inspection window shows both wire ends seated
Solid wirePIDG terminals & splices accept solid or stranded; FASTON is stranded only
✓ STAMPING QUIRK
22-16 AWG PIDG terminals are stamped “22-18” in accordance with MIL-T-7928, and must be crimped with the 22-18/22-16 (red) PIDG tooling. Don’t re-sort them as a different range.
