§03 Terminals & Splices · Sheet TS-03

PLASTI-GRIP · TERMINYL · CERTI-SEAL

SOURCE — AMP CATALOG 82042 · PP. 37–58

The other three families cover the ends of the spectrum PIDG doesn’t: cheap commercial terminations, big-copper power lugs, and sealed splices for wet or high-altitude environments.

PLASTI-GRIP
Commercial workhorse
Vinyl insulation · 90 °C · 600 V · UL486.
22 AWG – 2/0. Funnel entry & serrations on small sizes, dimples on 8–2/0. Terminals, butt & parallel splices — the inexpensive answer for almost every commercial application.
TERMINYL
Heavy copper · mil-spec
Nylon insulation · 105 °C · MIL-T-7928 Type II Class 2.
8 AWG – 4/0 — the power-lug range. Dimpled barrels, tool die marks stamp the wire size into the crimp for built-in QC. Airborne & ground-support duty.
CERTI-SEAL
Sealed window splice
Nylon splice, 24–10 AWG, for aircraft & missile wiring.
Metal rings crimp onto the wire insulation at both ends — a moisture-tight seal that holds even at altitude. Fits 100+ MIL-wire insulation diameters via ring adapters.

⚠ NYLON VS VINYL
The insulation material is the quickest family tell: nylon (PIDG, TERMINYL) is the higher-dielectric, higher-temperature choice; vinyl (PLASTI-GRIP) trades 15 °C of rating for cost. Vinyl also marks the oversize-expansion variants in the PIDG line.
