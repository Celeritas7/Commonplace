§03 Terminals & Splices · Sheet TS-05

Tongues & Splices

SOURCE — AMP CATALOG 82042 · TERMINAL & SPLICE SECTIONS THROUGHOUT

Every family repeats the same menu of tongue shapes. The barrel end is identical; the tongue is chosen by how the joint will be assembled, serviced and loaded.

FIG. TS-05 — THE TONGUE MENU
RING
Captive — can’t fall off the stud
SPADE / FORK
Slides under a loosened nut
FLANGED SPADE
Turned-up tips resist slipping out
RECTANGULAR
Flat tab for clamp-style connections
HOOK
Hooks on without full nut removal
SLOTTED RING
Ring security, fork convenience
FLAG
Tongue at 90° — tight spaces
RING · 90° BEND
Wire exits parallel to the panel
SPRING SPADE
Phosphor bronze — keeps tension

RULE — PREFER RING WHERE THE NUT COMES ALL THE WAY OFF; SPADE/HOOK ONLY WHERE SERVICE SPEED JUSTIFIES A NON-CAPTIVE JOINT.

TABLE TS-05 — SPLICE GEOMETRIES
SpliceGeometry & use
ButtWires enter opposite ends, meet at a centre stop — the standard in-line joint. Step-down adapter inserts join unequal sizes.
Butt windowButt splice with an inspection window — verify both stripped ends are seated before crimping (PIDG, CERTI-SEAL).
ParallelBoth wires enter the same end — pigtails, feed-throughs, and dead-ending a pair (PLASTI-GRIP).
Sealed windowCERTI-SEAL — butt window plus insulation-crimped seal rings for fluid and altitude exposure.
