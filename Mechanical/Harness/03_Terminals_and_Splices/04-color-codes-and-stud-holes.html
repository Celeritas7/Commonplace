Colour Codes & Stud Holes

SOURCE — AMP CATALOG 82042 · INSULATION COLOR CODE P. 4 · STUD HOLE SIZES P. 6

Two lookups happen before every termination: the insulation colour names the wire range (and therefore the tool die), and the stud size names the hole. Both exist to make the wrong combination visibly wrong.

✗ THE COLOUR TRAP
The red/blue/yellow cycle repeats up the size ladder — yellow means 26-22 and 12-10 and 4 and 2/0. Colour identifies range only within a size class; read the stamped size on the barrel before crimping big lugs.

✓ HOW TO USE IT
Pick the terminal listed for the stud on the drawing — the hole is cut ~.004–.026” over the stud diameter for a snug, non-rattling fit. A #5 stud (.125”) takes the #5 terminal (.129” hole), not a #6 “close enough”. Oversized holes shrink the bearing area under the nut and loosen under vibration.
