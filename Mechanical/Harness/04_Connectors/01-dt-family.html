§04 Connectors · Sheet DT-01

The Deutsch DT Family

SOURCE — DEUTSCH DTM 13/15 DATA SHEET · INDUSTRIAL PRODUCTS DIVISION

The Deutsch DT series is the field-proven sealed connector of off-highway and vehicle harnesses. The DTM line miniaturises it for signal-level work: size 20 contacts, improved sealing, same performance as DT — for the circuits where even slight signal degradation matters.

Housing
Thermoplastic
Moulded body with silicone mounting seal — the sealing system, not a potting compound, keeps the environment out.
Contacts
Molded-in copper alloy
Solid pins moulded into the header — excellent shock and vibration reliability. Tin plated; gold optional for low-level signals.
Mating
DTM06 plugs
Headers mate the DTM06 series wire-side plugs, terminated with solid or low-cost stamped-and-formed contacts.

ⓘ READING A PART NUMBER
DTM13-12P A → DTM series · 13 = right-angle header · 12 = positions · P = pins (headers) / S = sockets (plugs) · trailing letter = keying position, “A” standard.
