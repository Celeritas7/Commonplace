§04 Connectors · Sheet DT-02

DTM13 / 15 PCB Headers

SOURCE — DEUTSCH DTM 13/15 SERIES RIGHT ANGLE DATA SHEET

Both headers put the same sealed DTM interface on a circuit board; the choice is purely which way the harness leaves the enclosure. DTM13 turns the pins 90° for a low-profile side exit; DTM15 runs them straight through for a face exit.

✓ WHY MOLDED-IN PINS
No crimp joint between board and contact — the pin is part of the header. Under shock and vibration there is nothing to fret or back out; the failure modes move to the solder joint, which you can inspect.
✗ DON’T GUESS THE FOOTPRINT
PCB hole pattern and pin positions are consult-factory data on this sheet — pull the current envelope drawing before laying out a board; the 2001 dimensions may have been revised.
