§04 Connectors · Sheet DT-04

Sealing, Mating & Keying

SOURCE — DEUTSCH DTM SHEET · CALTRANS ELECTRIC STANDARD §2.5 CONNECTORS

Connector rules exist for the day someone reconnects things wrong in the dark, in the rain. The Caltrans electric standard reduces them to a checklist any harness must pass; the DTM keying options are how a Deutsch design passes the mismating rule mechanically.

TABLE DT-04 — THE CONNECTOR CHECKLIST (CALTRANS §2.5)
RuleRequirement
SealingExposed to weather → IP65. Can be submerged → IP67.
MismatingSimilar connectors in close proximity must be keyed, coded or marked so the wrong pair can’t be joined.
LockingConnectors shall lock together — friction fit is not retention.
Dead pinsExposed pin contacts must not be energised after disconnect — power lives on the socket side.
GreaseDielectric compound is a backup, never the primary seal — but running/stop/turn-light connectors do get it.
Point-to-pointSeparable harnesses use two connectors, same contact count, wired pin 1→1, A→A — no re-mapping inside a cable.
GrommetsOne wire per grommet hole in a sealed connector.
SlackEvery lead lands with strain-relief slack — no tension or shear at the contact.

✓ DESIGN HABIT
Assign keying positions on the schematic, not at the bench — the position letter is part of the part number (“Position A supplied as standard”), so two neighbouring connectors of the same size should never share a letter.
