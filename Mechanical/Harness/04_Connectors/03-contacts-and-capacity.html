§04 Connectors · Sheet DT-03

Contacts & Capacity

SOURCE — DEUTSCH DTM 13/15 DATA SHEET · MATERIALS & MATING NOTES

A connector is only as good as the metal touching metal inside it. Three contact decisions travel with every DTM circuit: size (sets current), construction (solid vs stamped), and plating (tin vs gold).

20
Contact size
The DTM contact — sized for 20 AWG-class signal wiring. (The bigger DT family carries size 16.)
7.5 A
Per contact
Rated capacity of the size 20 contact — plenty for sensors and data, not for motor or lamp feeds.
Sn/Au
Plating
Tin plated as standard; gold optional (consult factory) where micro-amp signals can’t tolerate tin-oxide film.

⚠ MATCH THE WIRE TO THE GROMMET
The rear wire seal grips one insulation diameter range. A wire too thin leaks; too thick splits the seal. Pick wire from the §02 tables whose finished OD lands in the connector’s seal range — and one wire per grommet hole, never two.
