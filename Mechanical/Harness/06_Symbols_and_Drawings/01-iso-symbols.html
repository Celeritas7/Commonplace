ISO Symbol System

SOURCE — ISO/FDIS 3287 · OPERATOR SYMBOLS, BASIC SET §6 & COMBINATIONS

ISO 3287 symbols are a grammar, not a picture book: a dozen basic shapes name the systems and states, and real indicator symbols are built by combining them. Learn the twelve and you can read any combination on a panel or drawing.

FIG. SD-01b — THE COMBINATION RULE
ENGINE + OIL + FILTER = ENGINE OIL FILTER · 10.3

Section 10 of the standard builds every engine indicator this way: engine + oil = lubricating oil; add a pressure arrow (10.1 · reg. 1374), the level triangle (10.2 · 1373) or the filter bars (10.3 · 1376). Direction arrows (§9) mark operating direction — forward/reverse (1436) or multiple direction (1703).

✓ REGISTRATION NUMBERS
Every symbol carries an ISO/IEC registration number (engine 1156, transmission 1166, filter 1369, temperature 0034, on 5007, off 5008…). Cite the number on drawings — it is unambiguous where a sketch is not.
✗ DON’T FREESTYLE
A near-miss symbol is worse than text: “!” means failure/malfunction (1603), not “caution”; the bar and circle are ON and OFF specifically. If the standard has no combination for your case, write words.
