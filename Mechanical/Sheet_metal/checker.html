      How it reads the part

      Geometry

      What it recognises

      • Thickness: the most common gap between opposite flat faces.
      • Bends: pairs of cylinders on one axis whose radii differ by t.
      • Holes: full cylinders along the sheet normal. Slots: two half-cylinders.
      • Assemblies: each solid is listed; pick the one to check.
      Measurement

      How distances are taken

      • Flange height: rays from each bend's tangent line across the flat, shortest one wins.
      • Hole to bend: from the hole edge to the inside face of the next leg.
      • Relief: probes the flat beside each end of every bend.
      Limits

      What it does not check

      • Louvres, embosses, extruded holes, countersinks, hems and other formed features.
      • Corner reliefs where two flanges meet, and bend sequence collisions.
      • Tolerances, welds and hardware. Treat a pass as "no obvious problems", then confirm with the shop.
